What to tell us

A few details help us give you a useful answer on the first call.

  • What you need: network, cabling, Wi-Fi, website or support
  • Where it is, or where the work would happen
  • Roughly how many people and devices are involved
  • What's going wrong, or what you want to build

FAQ

Common questions

Do you have prices on the website?

Not yet. Every job is different, so we quote after we understand what you need. That way you get a price that fits the work.

Do you work with individuals as well as businesses?

Yes. Most of our work is for businesses, but we also help individuals with home and office networks. Tell us what you need when you call.

Where do you work?

Remote help works wherever your network is. On-site work depends on the location, so tell us where you are when you call.

Can you help with an existing network, not just new installs?

Yes. Troubleshooting and support plans are often for networks that are already in place.
